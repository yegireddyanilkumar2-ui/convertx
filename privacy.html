Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

🔒 The short version: ConvertX processes all files locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. We do not store, read, or share your documents, images, or videos.

1. Who We Are

ConvertX is a free, browser-based file conversion tool available at convert.aiusagetools.com. ConvertX is operated as part of the AIUsageTools suite of free productivity tools.

2. Files You Convert

All file processing on ConvertX happens entirely within your web browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. This means:

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers
  • Your files are never stored anywhere outside your device
  • Your files are never read, accessed, or shared by ConvertX or any third party
  • File data exists only in your browser's memory during conversion and is discarded when you close the tab

This applies to all file types: PDFs, images (JPG, PNG, WebP), video files, and any other format processed by ConvertX tools.

3. Information We Collect

ConvertX collects minimal data necessary to operate and improve the service:

  • Usage analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about page visits, time on site, browser type, and general geographic region. This data contains no personally identifiable information and no file data.
  • Error logs: Browser errors may be logged anonymously to help us improve tool reliability. No file content is included in error logs.

We do not collect your name, email address, IP address (beyond what Google Analytics anonymizes), or any account information.

4. Cookies

ConvertX uses the following cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid): Used for anonymous usage statistics. These cookies do not identify you personally.
  • Google AdSense cookies: Used to serve relevant advertisements. Google may use cookies to show personalized ads based on your browsing history.

You can opt out of personalized Google advertising at adssettings.google.com. You can also disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

ConvertX does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The service is intended for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our site, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Security

Because ConvertX processes files locally in your browser and does not transmit file data to any server, your documents, images, and videos are inherently protected. We cannot access your files because they never reach us.
