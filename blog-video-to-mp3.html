Why Extract Audio from Video?

Video files are large. Audio is all you sometimes need. Here are the most common reasons people convert video to MP3:

Output Format Guide

MP3

Universal compatibility. Works on every device, player, and platform. Best for music, podcasts, and general sharing.

WAV

Lossless audio. Larger file size but perfect quality. Best for professional audio work and editing.

OGG

Open-source format. Excellent quality at small file sizes. Best for web apps and games.

AAC

Apple's standard. Better quality than MP3 at the same bitrate. Best for Apple devices and iTunes.

Choosing the Right Bitrate

128 kbps

Good for speech, podcasts, and interviews. The file size is small and voice quality is clear. Not ideal for music where you want to hear full audio detail.

192 kbps

The sweet spot for most uses. Music sounds good, file sizes stay manageable, and it plays perfectly on any device. This is the recommended setting for general use.

320 kbps

Maximum quality for MP3. Use this when audio quality matters most — music production, professional recordings, or archiving. Files are roughly 2.5× larger than 128 kbps.

Video to MP3 vs. Other Audio Extractors

Most online video-to-MP3 tools work by uploading your video to their server, extracting the audio on their end, and sending it back. This creates several problems:

  • Your private video content is on someone else's server
  • Large files take a long time to upload before processing even starts
  • Many sites have file size caps (typically 100MB–500MB)
  • You may hit daily conversion limits
  • Some tools add audio watermarks or degrade quality

ConvertX uses the Web Audio API and FFmpeg.wasm (WebAssembly port of FFmpeg) to run audio extraction directly in your browser. Your video stays on your device. Conversion speed depends on your CPU, not a server queue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a file size limit?

No server-side limit. Your browser handles everything locally. Very large files (2GB+) may be slow depending on your device, but there's no hard cap.

Will the audio quality match the original?

Yes, up to the quality already present in the source video. If the original video has low-quality audio, the MP3 will reflect that. ConvertX doesn't add degradation.

Can I extract audio from a protected video?

ConvertX only processes files you provide directly. It cannot download or bypass DRM-protected content from streaming services.

How long does conversion take?

A typical 1-hour MP4 at 1080p converts to MP3 in 1–3 minutes on a modern laptop. The process runs on your CPU, so faster devices convert faster.
